#### Full Description:
> **Passport Photo Studio Pro** is a fast, powerful, and privacy-first Windows desktop suite designed for students, job applicants, cyber cafes, photographers, and professionals.
>
> 🚀 **THREE POWERFUL MODULES IN ONE APP:**
>
> 1. **Passport Photo Studio**
> • Official passport, visa, and ID presets: 35×45 mm (India/UK/Schengen), 2×2 inch / 51×51 mm (US/OCI), 30×40 mm, and custom dimensions.
> • Biometric face alignment and guideline indicators (chin, eye line, crown).
> • Background replacement (Pure White, Studio Off-White, Light Blue, Light Grey).
> • Automatic print sheet layouts on 4×6 inch (fits 8 photos) and A4 paper (fits up to 30+ photos) with cutting lines and gap controls.
> • Export as high-resolution JPEG, PNG, or print-ready PDF.
>
> 2. **Document Scanner Studio**
> • 4-corner manual perspective warp with 8-parameter homography math: Straighten skewed mobile phone scans instantly.
> • Professional document enhancements: Crisp Black & White, Grayscale, Color Boost, Magic Contrast, and Invert.
> • Dual-page A4 Front + Back ID layout: Place Aadhaar, PAN card, Driving License front and back side-by-side on a single sheet.
> • One-click PDF document creation with zero quality loss.
>
> 3. **Photo & Document Resizer Pro**
> • Target KB Compression: Compress any photo or signature to exact sizes (e.g. Under 50 KB, 20 KB to 50 KB, or 100 KB) with smart binary search optimization.
> • 0% to 100% Quality Slider with live, verified byte calculations (1 KiB = 1024 B).
> • Manual crop tool with 8 drag handles, zoom, and aspect ratio presets.
> • Ready-made Indian recruitment exam presets: SSC CGL/CHSL, UPSC Civil Services, IBPS Bank PO/Clerk, State PSCs, Railway RRB, and NTA NEET/JEE.
> • PDF Document Resizer & Compressor with structural reload validation.
>
> 🔒 **100% PRIVACY & SECURITY GUARANTEE:**
