# Privacy Policy for Passport Photo Studio Pro

**Effective Date:** October 31, 2026  
**Developer:** Kishan Kumar ([@kkrkishan](https://github.com/kkrkishan))  
**Application:** Passport Photo Studio Pro (Android & Windows)  
**Package Name:** `com.kkrkishan.passportphotostudio`

---

## 1. Overview
Passport Photo Studio Pro is an all-in-one biometric passport photo maker, document scanner, and image resizer application available for Android (Google Play Store) and Windows (Microsoft Store). We are deeply committed to safeguarding user privacy. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning user data, photos, and scanned identity documents.

---

## 2. 100% Offline & Local Processing Guarantee
- **No Cloud Uploads**: All photo editing, biometric facial alignment, cropping, background adjustments, AI portrait cutout, background blur, document scanning, perspective corrections, image compression, DPI configuration, and PDF generation are executed strictly **locally on your device**.
- **No Remote Transmission**: Your original photos, cropped pictures, signatures, scanned identity cards, and exported documents **never leave your personal device**. They are never uploaded or transmitted to any external server or cloud service.

---

## 3. Data Collection and Usage
- **Personal Data**: Passport Photo Studio Pro does **NOT** collect, request, harvest, track, or store any personal data, names, email addresses, or identification numbers.
- **Biometric Data**: Any face alignment or cropping guides in the software are processed solely in temporary device memory (RAM) while you edit. No facial templates, biometric models, or biometric hashes are collected or shared.
- **Analytics & Tracking**: We do **NOT** use any third-party tracking libraries, telemetry SDKs, Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or advertising networks.

---

## 4. Device Permissions (Android)
The application requests only the essential runtime permissions strictly required to perform local photo editing tasks:
- **Camera (`CAMERA`)**: Used solely when you choose to capture a live photo for your passport picture or document scan using your device's camera. Camera data is processed locally in RAM and is never broadcast or streamed.
- **Photos / Media Storage (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / MediaStore)**: Used solely to let you choose an existing photo from your gallery to edit, and to save your final 300 DPI passport photos or A4 print sheets directly to your device's Gallery / Downloads folder.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
Passport Photo Studio Pro does not collect or solicit information from anyone under the age of 13. The application is family-friendly, ad-free, and safe for users of all ages.
